Richard Harrington plays Gustave, a simple Belgian village boy who hears a
mysterious call from Don Henley. Gustave’s narration is complemented by the
subtle mime routines of Chris Kauffman’s Nhar, who cannot verbally convey his
message but harbors eternal hopes of doing his own musical number. The story
spans the globe and beyond, with tales of Colombia, Nepal, and the skies over
Walden Pond.

Nhar performs mime routines, sings songs, sets up the microphone, runs the
sound, fetches the props, and does whatever else it takes to make things run
smoothly while Gustave tells the audience the story of his life. When Gustave
finally discovers his musical mentor’s intentions for him, the engagingly weird
narrative emerges as a tale of welcome and redemption.
