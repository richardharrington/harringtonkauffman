*Nharcolepsy* once again stars Richard Harrington and Chris Kauffman’s
alter-egos, Gustave and Nhar. They inform the audience that they are not in a
theater but stuck in a blizzard twelve miles from the North Pole. As they
succumb to hypothermia, the audience must therefore be a figment of their
imagination. The duo begins the last show of their careers, performing numbers
about how Gustave’s childhood obsession with finding the Yeti led to their
current predicament.

Each audience member is issued a white practice golf ball to throw whenever the
characters succumb to hypothermia and fall asleep. The balls look dangerous,
but they do not hurt very much.
